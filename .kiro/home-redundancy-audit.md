1. Hero Slide 1 details: "Currently seeking full-time Data Engineering roles..."
2. Hero Slide 2 details: "Currently seeking full-time Data Engineering positions..."
3. Hero Slide 3 details: "Currently seeking full-time Data Engineering opportunities..."

5. Results section banner: "Currently Seeking Full-Time Data Engineering Roles"

- ✅ Hero Slide 1 (primera impresión)

- Service cards

- 20-25% menos texto
- 40% más claridad
