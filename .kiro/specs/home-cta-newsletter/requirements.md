# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Enhancement of the home page to improve user engagement through better call-to-action elements and a newsletter/contact capture section. This feature aims to increase conversion rates and lead generation while maintaining the current clean design aesthetic.

## Glossary

- **CTA_System**: The call-to-action system that guides users toward desired actions
- **Newsletter_Component**: The email capture component for lead generation
- **Home_Page**: The main landing page of the website
- **Contact_Teaser**: A brief contact invitation section

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a potential client visiting the website, I want clear and prominent call-to-action buttons, so that I can easily take the next step to engage with the services.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a user views the hero section, THE CTA_System SHALL display prominent action buttons with contrasting colors
2. WHILE browsing the home page, THE CTA_System SHALL provide multiple engagement opportunities without being intrusive
3. THE CTA_System SHALL include primary actions like "Get Started", "Contact Me", and "View Portfolio"
4. WHERE users scroll through services, THE CTA_System SHALL offer relevant next steps for each service area
5. THE CTA_System SHALL maintain visual hierarchy with the most important actions being most prominent

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a visitor interested in staying updated, I want to easily subscribe to a newsletter, so that I can receive valuable insights about data engineering and industry trends.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Newsletter_Component SHALL capture email addresses with a simple, single-field form
2. WHEN a user submits their email, THE Newsletter_Component SHALL provide immediate feedback confirmation
3. THE Newsletter_Component SHALL include a compelling value proposition for subscribing
4. THE Newsletter_Component SHALL be positioned strategically without disrupting the main content flow
5. WHERE users interact with the newsletter form, THE Newsletter_Component SHALL validate email format before submission

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a business owner looking for data engineering services, I want a quick way to get in touch, so that I can discuss my project needs without navigating through multiple pages.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Contact_Teaser SHALL provide a direct path to initiate contact
2. WHEN users want to reach out, THE Contact_Teaser SHALL offer multiple contact methods (email, form, calendar)
3. THE Contact_Teaser SHALL include a brief value proposition about working together
4. THE Contact_Teaser SHALL be visually distinct but complementary to the overall design
5. WHERE appropriate, THE Contact_Teaser SHALL include social proof or credibility indicators
