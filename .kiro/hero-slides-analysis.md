## 🔍 ANÁLISIS COMPLETO DE LOS 3 SLIDES

### **SLIDE 1**: "Data Engineer | Open to Full-Time Opportunities"

**Subtitle actual**:
```
"10+ years professional experience: Construction PM → Quant Trader → Data Engineer. 
Building systems where reliability isn't optional. 
98%+ uptime, <500ms latency, 2TB+ daily processing."
```

**Details actual**:
```
"Building reliable data infrastructure for teams where downtime isn't an option. 
Combining construction engineering discipline with trading floor urgency."
```

#### 📊 EVALUACIÓN:
- ✅ **Primera parte FUERTE**: "Construction PM → Quant Trader → Data Engineer" (único)
- ⚠️ **"Building systems where reliability isn't optional"** - GENÉRICO (cualquier DE diría esto)
- ⚠️ **"downtime isn't an option"** - CLICHÉ (frase que todos usan)
- ⚠️ **"construction engineering discipline"** - BUZZWORD (abstracto)
- ⚠️ **"trading floor urgency"** - BUZZWORD (abstracto)

**VEREDICTO**: 🟡 **50% FUERTE / 50% GENÉRICO**


- ✅ "I have. That's the difference." - DIRECTO Y CONFIADO
